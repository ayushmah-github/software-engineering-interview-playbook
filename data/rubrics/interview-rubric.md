# Interview evaluation rubric

This rubric is intended for self-assessment and peer feedback.

## Categories

| Category | What to look for | Score (1-5) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem framing | Clarifies assumptions and scope | 1-5 |
| Technical correctness | Reasoning is sound and grounded | 1-5 |
| Trade-off analysis | Explains choices and alternatives | 1-5 |
| Communication | Clear, organized, concise answers | 1-5 |
| Ownership | Shows initiative and accountability | 1-5 |
| Learning | Reflects on mistakes and improvements | 1-5 |

## Scoring guidance

- 1: unclear, weak, or missing
- 2: limited evidence
- 3: acceptable but inconsistent
- 4: strong and well-supported
- 5: excellent and highly structured

## Feedback template

- what was clear
- where the reasoning broke down
- what trade-off was not fully explained
- what to improve next time

## Example review

"You framed the problem well, but your backend trade-off discussion needed a clearer explanation of read/write patterns and consistency constraints. Next time, explicitly compare the candidate options before choosing one."
