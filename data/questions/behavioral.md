# Behavioral interview questions

Use these to practice structured, outcome-oriented responses.

## Questions

1. Tell me about a time you had to resolve a conflict in a team.
2. Describe a project where you had to work under ambiguity.
3. Tell me about a time you had to make a trade-off between speed and correctness.
4. Describe a situation where you influenced others without authority.
5. Give an example of a time you improved a process or system.
6. Tell me about a time you made a mistake and how you fixed it.
7. Describe a time when you had to align stakeholders with different priorities.
8. Tell me about a time you handled a high-pressure production issue.
9. Give an example of a project where you led technical direction.
10. Describe a time you delivered value with incomplete information.

## Good response pattern

Use the STAR structure:

- Situation
- Task
- Action
- Result

Focus on clarity, ownership, and measurable outcomes.
