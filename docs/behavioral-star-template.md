# STAR interview template

Use this framework to structure behavioral answers clearly and consistently.

## Situation

Describe the context briefly.

- what was happening
- what the team or project was trying to achieve
- why it mattered

## Task

Explain your responsibility.

- what was expected of you
- what you were accountable for
- what constraints affected your work

## Action

Describe the specific actions you took.

- what you did first
- what decisions you made
- how you collaborated with others
- what trade-offs you weighed

## Result

Summarize the outcome with evidence.

- what changed
- what metrics or signals mattered
- what you learned
- what you would do differently next time

## Example

**Situation:** A team was shipping a backend feature with unstable deployment timing and unclear ownership.

**Task:** I was responsible for resolving the release bottlenecks and making the deployment process more predictable.

**Action:** I mapped the release steps, identified the handoff issues, introduced a small checklist, and aligned the team around ownership and rollback criteria.

**Result:** Deployments became more predictable, blockers were reduced, and the team had clearer visibility into release status.

## Tips

- keep answers focused and concrete
- avoid storytelling without a clear outcome
- quantify where possible
- emphasize judgment and trade-offs, not just activity
